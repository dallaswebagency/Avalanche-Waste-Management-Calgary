Why choose Avalanche

Meet Avalanche

  1. Honesty & integrity

    We value business integrity and being upfront with every customer, from the first call to pick-up.

  2. Fair pricing

    The price of the roll-off bin, along with the dump fees and handling fees, is what you pay for.

  3. No hidden fees

    No fuel surcharge, no environmental fees, no cost recovery charges. A rare promise in this industry.

  4. Quality service, always

    We aim to fill all orders within 24 hours, and we handle on-site relocations, last-minute orders and after-hours requests.

  5. Environmental responsibility

    We recycle and reuse where possible, and our junk removal bins are manufactured in Southern Alberta.

Green initiatives

A full dumpster is a chance to make something new

Avalanche cares about the environment. Most of the junk we haul is recycled or reused, and we aim to keep waste out of landfills.

  • Recycling and reuse wherever possible
  • Diverting waste from landfills
  • Bins manufactured in Southern Alberta

How it works
